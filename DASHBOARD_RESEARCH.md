# Dashboard research notes

Apple Human Interface Guidelines applied to this redesign:

- Layout: establish visual hierarchy, align related elements, group related information, use consistent spacing, and progressively disclose secondary detail.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout
- Charts: use clear titles and units, concise labels, a visible summary of the main message, accessible descriptions, and do not rely on color alone.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/charts
- Color: use semantic color consistently, preserve contrast across appearances, and use text or shape in addition to color for meaning.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color
- Lists and tables: keep row text succinct, use descriptive headings, and provide clear selected/sorted feedback.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/lists-and-tables
- Toolbars: keep actions deliberate, group them by function, keep one primary action, and move lower-priority actions into overflow.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars
